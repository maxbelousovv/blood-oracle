# Changelog

## 0.5.0

### Loresheets
- **A Loresheets section** on the Merits & flaws tab. Each loresheet is a card with its five levels: taken ones bright, the rest dimmed; hovering a level shows its text.
- **The detail panel** shows the start of the description and every level with Take or Remove. **Full rules** opens the whole loresheet.
- **Private loresheets** are hidden from anyone but the character's owners and the Storyteller.
- **The picker** searches 151 loresheets by name, level and restriction. Loresheets outside the character's clan, blood or Blood Sorcery are dimmed and listed after the rest, but can still be taken.
- **Each level is an ordinary merit** to the system, rated at its level, so the system's sheet and experience see it.
- **Texts are a private file** (`blood-oracle-loresheets.json`) generated by `tools/extract-loresheets.mjs`; names and restrictions ship with the module. See RULES.md.

### Rules reference
- **One file.** `tools/build-reference.mjs` builds the rules, loresheets, tooltips and coterie texts into a single `blood-oracle-rules.json` from a folder of sources, picking the newest version of each table. The module reads every section from it, and still reads the old separate files when a section is missing. See REFERENCE.md (Russian) and RULES.md.
- **Resolve** has its tooltip.

### Character creation
- **A wizard for vampires**, in its own window. The first time a blank vampire's sheet is opened it asks: **Character creator** or **By hand**. Choosing by hand is remembered and never asked again; closing the question asks again next time. Characters that already have ratings are never asked. The creator is also in the sheet window's menu (⋮), with a warning when the character already has ratings. Ten steps: clan, attributes, skills, specialties, generation, predator type, basics, disciplines, touchstones, merits and flaws. Each step's Confirm waits until the step follows the rules; the side panel shows the character so far.
- **The draft is kept on the character** between sessions, with a **Continue creation** button in the header while it is unfinished, and written to the sheet only by **Finish**, so an unfinished character never leaves the sheet half-filled.
- **Rules:** attributes 4/3/3/3/2/2/2/2/1; three skill distributions; required specialties for Academics, Craft, Performance and Science plus one free; generation and stage in one list (childer, neonate, ancilla — ancillae get +2 advantages, +2 flaws, −1 Humanity); the predator type's choices, applied through its usual grants; two dots and one in clan disciplines plus the predator's dot, one power per dot; 7 advantage and 2 flaw points, loresheets included.
- **Powers fit their dots:** each dot takes a power of its own level or lower — three dots mean one power of level 1, one up to 2, one up to 3. The same holds for new dots in the level-up.
- **Caitiff** choose any three disciplines, begin with Suspect on top of their flaws, and cannot buy Status. **Thin-bloods** have no predator type and no clan disciplines: they take thin-blood merits and flaws in equal numbers, and Thin-blood Alchemist brings Alchemy and a first formula.
- **Bonus experience** from the stage goes onto the sheet unspent.

### Level-up
- **Spend…** in the Experience section opens a level-up window: every attribute, skill, discipline and advantage with steppers, specialties, Blood Sorcery rituals, Oblivion ceremonies, thin-blood formulas, Blood Potency (up to the generation's cap), new backgrounds, merits and loresheet levels, and — for a coterie member — the coterie's domain and advantages.
- **Costs from the corebook table**, shown in the window: each new level paid in turn (Strength 2 → 4 is 15 + 20); disciplines × 5 in clan, × 7 otherwise, × 6 for Caitiff; 3 per specialty; level × 3 per ritual, ceremony or formula; 3 per dot of advantage, loresheet level or coterie dot; Blood Potency × 10.
- **A power for every new discipline dot** is picked in the window. Spending past what the character has is allowed, with a warning in the confirmation. One entry in the experience journal records the whole purchase.
- Investing in a coterie needs ownership of the coterie actor; without it the window says to ask the Storyteller.

### Specialties
- **No more specialties than dots** in a skill — on the sheet, in character creation and in the level-up.

### Coterie
- **Coterie types.** 15 types from the corebook on the group sheet. Choosing one raises the domain to its ratings and adds its backgrounds and flaws, marked "type"; choices ("Allies or Mawla", Regency's spread of 10 dots and matching flaws) are made in a dialog. Changing the type takes back exactly what it gave. Possible extras open the picker on themselves. A custom type can be named instead.
- **Coterie advantages.** Backgrounds from the corebook's coterie list and any background flaw, with details and a picker, replacing the generic list for coteries.
- **Coterie merits and flaws.** The picker also offers 4 coterie merits, 6 coterie flaws and 18 domain merits under Chasse, Lien and Portillon, each with its resonance. They ship with the module, so the picker works without the rules reference; descriptions come from the private coterie file.
- **Clan coterie merits.** 16 clan merits (Call to Purpose, Boot and Rally…), offered in the coterie picker and, for a character, in their own merit picker for their clan only. A one-line summary leads the picker preview and appears when the pointer rests on a coterie merit's name.
- **Rules reference.** A Rules button on the coterie advantages section shows the rulebook's notes on coterie advantages and on Merits of Membership, from the private coterie file.
- **Domain in rolls.** A coterie member's roll dialog has a Domain block: Lien adds its dice for dealing with locals and investigating in the domain; "Hunting in the domain" sets the difficulty from Chasse (6 at one dot, one less per dot) and turns Lien off, which never helps hunting; Portillon is shown as a note for the Storyteller.
- **Secret members.** An eye on each member of a coterie marks the membership secret: that character gets neither the coterie's advantages nor its domain, and their sheet says so.
- **Shared with members.** A member's Merits & flaws tab shows what their coterie holds, read-only; it follows membership.
- **Descriptions are a private file** (`blood-oracle-coterie.json`) generated by `tools/extract-coterie-types.mjs`. See RULES.md.

### Art
- **Discipline art.** A thumbnail in the corner of the discipline panel, with the overview flowing around it; clicking opens Foundry's image window with Show to Players. Files live in a private folder, `Data/blood-oracle/art/disciplines/<key>.webp`, beside the rules reference. See RULES.md.

### Attribute and skill tooltips
- **Hover tooltips.** Resting the pointer on an attribute or skill name for a second shows what it covers and its usual specialties. The texts are a private file (`blood-oracle-traits.json`) generated by `tools/extract-traits.mjs`. See RULES.md.
- **Coterie domain.** Chasse, Lien and Portillon on the group sheet get the same tooltip, from the same file; without it, a short summary.

## 0.4.0

### Detective board
- **Drawing lines.** Pressing a card's link button starts a line at once; it follows the pointer and snaps to the card under it. Drag onto a card and let go, or click and then click any card.
- **Anywhere on a card.** A line can end on any part of the target card — title, text, picture, buttons. While a line is out, card buttons and fields are disabled, so finishing a line can no longer press a card's × by accident.
- **Line controls on hover.** Hovering a line shows a × at each end, either of which removes it, and a + in the middle to add a label.
- **Labels are optional.** A line without a label shows nothing on it. A label has its own × that removes the label and keeps the line; clicking the label edits it.

### Specialties
- **Several per skill.** The skill row shows the first specialty and a "+N" chip; hovering lists them all in a styled tooltip.
- **Specialty popover.** Clicking opens a popover by the row: click to rename, × to remove, a field at the bottom to add. Read-only when the sheet is locked.
- **Predator specialties** cannot be removed or renamed there; their tooltip says to change the predator type instead.
- **One record at a time.** Edits now change one bonus record at a time and leave the rest of the skill's bonuses untouched.

### Pool constructor
- **Specialty chips.** The specialty checkbox is replaced by a row of chips: None plus every specialty of every skill in the pool, grouped by skill when there are two. The chat card names the one used: "Specialty (Grappling) +1".
- **Odds.** A new Odds button shows exact chances for the current pool: win, critical win, messy critical, bestial failure, total failure, and a table by number of successes. Beside the dialog it stays open and updates as the pool and difficulty change. Willpower rerolls are not counted.
- **Footer.** Modifier and Difficulty are steppers next to the buttons; Cancel and Roll are the same size.
- **Blood surge** turns red when ticked.

### Inventory
- **Armour dialog.** Armour is added and edited through a dialog like weapons: four classes from the corebook table with examples and drawbacks (0 against bullets, −1 die to Dexterity pools). The rating shows beside the name in the list.
- **Older armour.** Armour made without a class is read from its rating when that is unambiguous.
- **Class lists.** Weapon and armour class lists are left-aligned, with larger, upright examples.

### Look and feel
- **One note size.** Every small italic note — specialties, merit details, toggle notes, picker and dialog notes — now uses one size, set by `--bo-size-note`.
- **Checkboxes.** They no longer show Foundry's grey square over the sheet's own, everywhere on the sheet.

### Fixes
- Rolling with a skill in the pool no longer fails with "skillSlot is not defined".

## 0.3.0

- **Predator type bonuses:** granted with a choice dialog, tracked in a journal, rolled back when the predator changes.
- **Derived trackers:** Health and Willpower are derived from attributes.
- **Rolls:** opposed pools for powers, and the Remorse check.
- **Merit picker:** browse by rubric; Background flaws are listed and written as flaws.
- **Weapon dialog** with damage classes.
- **Unavailable powers** greyed out: amalgam requirements, rituals by Blood Sorcery, ceremonies by Oblivion.
- **Dropdowns** drawn in the sheet's own style, with search and keyboard support.
- **Pool constructor:** grouped by heading, with dots and untrained skills listed last.
